QRaft crafts custom QR codes entirely on your phone. There is no INTERNET permission, no account, and no analytics.

Type a website, Wi-Fi, vCard, or text payload. Style modules and finder eyes. Save a Website profile. Pin a Glance widget on the home screen or a LineageOS lock-screen panel. Tap to a full-screen bright QR. Set a safe-zone wallpaper that keeps finder patterns inside the zoom crop. Export PNG, SVG, PDF, or JSON.
